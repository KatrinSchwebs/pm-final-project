Future state journey, Digital instalment plan

Mara settles her claim on terms she can keep

Strategy: be transparent and answer everything in one stop, so a lasting instalment relationship becomes possible.
Value proposition
Help consumers agree to an affordable plan they can keep, without a call, while recovering more and sending agents only the cases that need them.
1

Arrive and See Everything

User action
Opens the SMS, email or letter QR link on her phone and sees all her claims.
Internal state
Mara: wary, short on time. System: claims consolidated, amounts reconciled with the letter.
Pain point addressed
One claim at a time; portal amount differs from the letter (T-202, T-203).
Action → Benefit
Show all open claims together → Mara sees her total, no guessing
Match portal amount to the letter → Removes the reason to call
2

Understand and Share

User action
Reads why income is asked, then enters her figures without leaving the page.
Internal state
Mara: cautious about data use. System: inputs stored once, retention period shown.
Pain point addressed
Unexplained income question; fear data is used against her (T-206, N-01, N-06).
Action → Benefit
Explain why income is asked, how long kept → Mara answers instead of leaving
Ask for this month or a normal month → Shift income is captured accurately
Keep answers when she goes back → No retyping, no lost progress
3

Choose a Plan She Can Keep

User action
Compares affordable options, sees missed-payment consequences, picks a payment date.
Internal state
Mara: gaining confidence. System: options pre-filtered by affordability, decision logic recorded.
Pain point addressed
Guessed plans, unknown consequences, no combined plan (N-02, T-202, T-208).
Action → Benefit
Check affordability before showing options → Plans match what Mara can pay
Show missed-payment consequences beside each plan → Mara commits knowing the risk
Offer one plan across claims → One payment, fewer letters, no call
Offer payment dates matching pay day → Fewer missed first instalments
4

Confirm and Keep It

User action
Confirms on her phone, gets a reminder before the first instalment, and pays.
Internal state
Mara: relieved, no call made. System: plan-kept tracked; only exceptions escalated with context.
Pain point addressed
Late reminders, blind agents, plans counted resolved on creation (T-207, T-205).
Action → Benefit
Send reminder before first due date → Fewer early missed payments
Track plan-kept rate beside SSR → Leadership sees outcomes, not just agreements
Send Daniel a summary on escalation → No repeated checks, faster resolution
Competitive advantages over the manual workaround
1

Answers where the question arises

Why income is asked, what a missed payment means and how claims combine appear on screen. Mara no longer leaves to search, ask an adviser or build a spreadsheet.
2

A plan calculated, not guessed

Options come from her own inputs and an affordability check, replacing a self-made number she cannot keep.
3

Outcomes anyone can verify

Plan-kept rate and agent summaries make results explainable to clients, compliance and Daniel. The manual workaround leaves no trace.
